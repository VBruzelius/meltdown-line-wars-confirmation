Clearer Late Survival Waves

Late Survival waves contain fewer enemies while keeping the same full-clear gold.

Balance

  • From Wave 21, regular waves contain at most 30 enemies and Swarm waves at most 40.
  • Enemy mixes stay proportional, with every intended type retained. HP, special traits and boss counts stay the same.
  • Gold from removed enemies moves into the wave-clear reward, preserving the full-clear gold total.
  • Wave 33 now sends 13 Steadfast, 13 Ironhide and 4 Nullborn instead of 42 enemies.

Fixes

  • Saved runs finish an already-active wave with its original enemies and clear reward. Following waves use the reduced counts.
