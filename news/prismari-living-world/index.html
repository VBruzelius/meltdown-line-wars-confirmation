Prismari Arrives

Build a crystal-powered Prismari army, explore livelier race backgrounds, and find clearer notifications in your Social Hub.

New

  • Play Prismari in Survival and human matchmaking, with a crystal-cavern hub and battlefield.
  • Build Resonant Cores and connect five conduit families. Their formations unlock 35 combinations with bolts, chains, pulses and lingering fields.
  • Upgrade fitted crystal models through four conduit tiers. Prismari uses General talents for this first TEST release; its race talent web will follow.
  • Diamond conduits reveal hidden enemies within 3 cells at every tier.
  • Choose from six initial Prismari profile portraits: the Resonant Core and five conduit families.

Improvements

  • Dawnforged golden flames and waterfalls, Ironclans fire and lava, and Dreadborn soulfire and mist now move subtly in hub and arena backgrounds.
  • Notifications use compact, distinct cards with sender information, read markers and relevant actions.
  • Friends, messages and notifications share the Social Hub, with portraits, levels and recent Survival and PvP records.
  • Prismari progression, loadouts, matchmaking and recorded results are supported by the TEST backend.
  • The shared per-race Survival and casual PvP XP allowance now matches the game's existing policy.

Known issues

  • Prismari currently uses General talents. Its dedicated race talent web will follow.
