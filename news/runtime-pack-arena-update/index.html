Smaller Downloads & Leaner Arenas

A smaller PC download preserves the current roster and artwork. Arena walls now share rendering work across repeated pieces.

Improvements

  • PC game packs store identical files once while retaining their resource paths and exact contents.
  • Retired model exports are omitted from player downloads. Current characters, equipment, animations and saved-run talent data remain available.
  • Walls, pillars, braziers and graveyard markers share rendering batches while preserving their artwork, layout and animated flames.

Fixes

  • Visual recovery uses the current character body instead of shipping the retired fitted roster.
  • Loading no longer requests retired T0 models or inactive arena banner and portal-frame parts.

Known issues

  • Dense Android combat still needs further performance work and real-device testing.
