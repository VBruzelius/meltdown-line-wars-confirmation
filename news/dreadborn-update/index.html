FACTION POWER

Every death makes the horde stronger.

Spread the plague

Infect the enemy wave with Disease. As infected enemies fall, the plague leaps to the next victim and grows more dangerous. One kill can ignite a chain reaction across the battlefield.

Make the dead fight again

Fallen infected enemies leave corpses behind. Your Corpsecallers turn those remains into devastating Shadow explosions. The bigger the battle, the more ammunition you leave on the ground.

SIX FAMILIES · FOUR UPGRADE TIERS

Choose how death strikes.

Begin with a Soulspark, evolve into one of six families, then forge your own path from T1 through T4. Each branch has its own combat identity—and the final tier brings its most fearsome form.

GRAVE KNIGHTS

Break their defenses.

Graveguards mark targets for punishment from the whole army. Death Knights carve away armor and spread infection through the crowd.

REAPERS

Harvest the frontline.

Wraithscythes sweep through packs, hitting harder against the diseased. Soulrend's relentless blades shred infected targets.

NECROMANCERS

Command the battlefield.

Bonebinders trap waves in sinister slowing fields. Corpsecallers make the fallen explode beneath the next assault.

LICHES

Unleash soulflame.

Soulflame Lancers pierce enemy lines with spectral bolts. Graveflame Revenants channel beams that grow deadlier the longer they hold their prey.

PLAGUEBEARERS

Let the infection loose.

Rotmaws spray disease across entire groups. Blight Catapults rain corrupted payloads that leave clouds of infection in their wake.

GRAVE ARCHERS

Death from a distance.

Blight Archers seed and strengthen infections from afar. Soulflame Archers pick off distant threats with punishing shadow-charged shots.

THE BATTLEFIELD

Welcome to the necropolis.

A graveyard-inspired arena, purple Dreadborn visuals and dark magical combat effects set the stage for your next Survival run.

NOW IN TEST

Will your defenses survive the dead?

Dreadborn is available for Survival testing. Discover the six families, experiment with upgrade paths and build deadly Disease-and-corpse combinations. Features and balance may evolve during TEST.
