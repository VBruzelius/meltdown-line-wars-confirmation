THE FACTION MECHANIC

One infection. A chain reaction.

Disease lasts 8 seconds and deals 1 Nature damage per stack, each second. Fresh infections start at one stack and can grow to a maximum of eight. Applying Disease again refreshes its duration without adding a stack or delaying its next damage tick.

Death spreads it

When an infected enemy dies, Disease jumps to the nearest living enemy within 3 cells and gains a stack. If the new target already has a stronger infection, that stronger infection stays.

A 3-stack infection jumping onto a 5-stack target stays at 5. The two stack counts are never added together.

Corpses become fuel

Enemies that die while infected leave a corpse for 8 seconds. Corpsecallers consume these remains to trigger Shadow explosions around them.

Each corpse can be consumed only once. Spread Disease first, then position your Corpsecallers to turn the fallen into damage against the next group.

COMPOSITION & POSITIONING

More ways to hold the line.

  • Bonebinder: channels a slowing damage zone for four seconds. The slow briefly lingers when an enemy leaves.
  • Graveflame Revenant: channels into 1 / 1 / 2 targets at T2 / T3 / T4. Each connection gains 25% of base DPS per second, reaching double base DPS after four seconds. Bosses take priority over elites, then regular enemies. Switching to a higher-priority target restarts that connection’s buildup.
  • Blight Catapult: targets beyond three cells, up to ten cells away. Its payload breaks into a two-second cloud that applies Disease; it does not add a separate poison DoT.
  • Soulrend: deals bonus damage to diseased enemies, rewarding a mixed army with reliable infection sources.
  • Soulflame Archer: gains bonus hit damage from at least four cells away. Give this path a long firing lane.
