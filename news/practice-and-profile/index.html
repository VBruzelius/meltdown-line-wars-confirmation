Practice Bots and a Better First Battle

Practice against bots, learn the basics with a guided tutorial, and enjoy clearer account and leaderboard controls.

New

  • Practice vs Bot adds Easy, Medium and Hard opponents for Live and Rounds Duel. Use your chosen race and loadout against a Dawnforged bot with matching talent point budgets. Practice grants no XP or rating.
  • New players can try a guided Survival tutorial, followed by an introduction to PvP sends and income. Replay it from Settings.
  • Meltdown Line Wars has a new silver and blue game icon. The launcher keeps its familiar orange icon.

Balance

  • Successfully sent enemies now return 50% of their purchase price after completing their route, rounded down. Cancelled sends retain their existing refunds.
  • Ranked winners can receive XP when an opponent surrenders after a genuine match. The quitter receives no XP; rating changes remain separate.

Improvements

  • Hub leaderboards use race emblems, whole-number scores and a more compact centered layout. Casual and Ranked choices have clearer icons.
  • Password recovery gives clearer sending and confirmation messages, including a reminder to check spam.

Fixes

  • Selected profile avatars restore correctly between sessions.
  • Inviting a friend to a party creates the party automatically when needed.

Known issues

  • Practice bots currently play Dawnforged. Hard is an initial test difficulty; Rounds practice can run longer than intended.
  • Physical phone and tablet performance testing of practice matches is still pending.
  • Prismari currently uses General talents. Its dedicated race talent web will follow.
