2026-11-01 · TEST

Ranked TEST and Smoother PvP

Try ranked Duel and King of the Hill, review hub leaderboards, and enjoy smoother PvP flow.

New

  • Open Survival and Ranked leaderboards directly from the hub.
  • Complete ten placements per queue to reveal your medal and rating.
  • Ranked TEST adds Duel and King of the Hill, with separate Live and Rounds ratings.
  • Queue Again returns you to matchmaking with the same game mode, race and send style.

Balance

  • PvP sending unlocks after the first income payment.
  • The first sender unit now costs 4 gold for 1 income, matching the baseline ratio of other sends.
  • Repeated matches against the same opponent no longer add an extra XP restriction. The shared daily race allowance still applies.
  • Mixed Cargo no longer grants starting income. Its first-three-type discounts remain, saving up to 6 gold per match.
  • Growing Convoy now grants +1 bonus income per 20 normal income bought through paid sends. Progress carries over without extra rounding benefits for cheap sends.
  • Prismari conduits now grant full power to every connected core. Linked cores also receive a 20% attack-power boost; existing saved runs retain their original rules.

Improvements

  • Runecarver buffs now show a glowing rune inscription beneath the buffed unit.
  • Account XP confirmation starts sooner after a match, with a pending indicator while the result is confirmed.
  • Prismari resonance stages and elemental attacks have stronger visual identities, with clearer lightning, frost, fire and heavy impacts and fewer overlapping ground effects.

Fixes

  • Returning from PvP clears the finished match so Play can be used again.
  • PvP batch upgrades allow the affordable part of a selection and leave the remaining units selected. Talent discounts are included in the affordability count.
  • More Info in upgrade confirmation expands and collapses without a checkbox.
  • Rounds income now arrives once at the round boundary. Live income remains every 15 seconds.
  • Android Tech upgrades respond correctly while Multi Select is enabled.
  • Windows game updates now prepare normal folder permissions before installation and show clearer access-error messages.

Known issues

  • Prismari currently uses General talents. Its dedicated race talent web will follow.
