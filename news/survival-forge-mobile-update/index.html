Survival, Forge & Mobile Improvements

Stronger Mauler paths, stacking armor traps and more late-wave gold help you adapt your army. Talent loadouts, building controls and mobile interfaces are clearer and easier to use.

Balance

  • Mauler cleave splash now deals 65% / 80% / 95% damage at T2 / T3 / T4.
  • Mauler single-target paths deal 3x damage to regular enemies and 5x to bosses. Their small secondary splash keeps its normal damage.
  • Armor reductions from different trappers now stack. Repeated hits from the same trapper refresh its own reduction.
  • Warded Survival enemies have half their previous health. Their magic resistance remains unchanged.
  • Survival clear rewards increase by 50% from Wave 21, giving more gold to adapt your maze.
  • Inquisitor-path damage vulnerability now scales with tier: +1 / +3 / +5.

Improvements

  • Talent Forge loadout tabs sit above the web, with clearer race and General point totals.
  • Banner aura surges have a visible effect so it is easier to see when The Rallying Beat activates.
  • Phone mode uses the same ornate Play buttons, race artwork and match-control grouping as PC.
  • The phone Menu button opens the centered shared Match Menu. Tech upgrades stay beside their status chip.
  • Unread messages use a red numbered badge in the hub and during matches.
  • Smaller model textures and lighter oversized weapons reduce asset memory and download size while preserving character bodies, rigs, animations and sharp interface text.
  • Weapon effects use less repeated rendering work. Floating damage numbers share a text layer on Android.
  • PC retains its original model textures and rendering detail.
  • Crowded Android battles batch flame layers and fire trails, reduce repeated ground-effect updates, and use a smaller 3D render budget while keeping interface text sharp.

Fixes

  • Quick Build retries your pending cells when enough gold arrives or a blocking placement is removed.
  • Loadouts can be renamed correctly. Talent previews show the benefit of the next point instead of the maximum-rank bonus.
  • Game Updates can show the latest release notes even when there are no separate announcements.
  • Survival rankings correctly retain and compare each account's best run for each race.

Known issues

  • Android remains in testing. Dense combat still needs further performance work, and emulator results do not guarantee real-device performance.
