Spell Effect Settings and Prismari Visuals

Choose Low, Medium or High spell effects, explore new Prismari cavern mode art and avatars, and see clearer spell damage types.

Improvements

  • The two-Sapphire Permafrost Sweep now breathes a widening cone of blue-white frost mist instead of sweeping crystal streaks.
  • Permafrost Sweep damage, Chill, freeze and shatter behavior remain unchanged.
  • Prismari ability descriptions explain each spell concisely, with Physical, Magic or mixed damage labels and separate direct-hit and status damage. Conduits explicitly show that they do not attack.
  • Choose Low, Medium or High spell effects in hub or match Settings. Your choice is saved on this device; phones start at Medium and desktop starts at High.
  • Hub and in-game Settings both have a square Mute music checkbox on desktop and phone layouts. Unmute restores your previous volume, with sound effects kept separate.
  • Prismari arenas now have carved cavern-stone walls, antique-gold trim, teal inlays, crystal-topped pillars and a matching textured quartzite floor.
  • Lower settings reduce particles, trail layers and lightning detail while preserving attack warnings, ranges and damage timing. Ground effects also avoid repeated material updates.
  • Prismari Duel, King of the Hill and Survival cards now have crystal-cavern artwork.
  • Choose from eleven Prismari avatars: a neutral seed-golem, five empowered cores and five crystal conduits.
  • Diamond blade orbits, accelerated/tremor variants and Polar Corona now reach 2.5 cells so they hit paths outside a fully linked core. The visible orbit matches its damage area; damage per hit and attack cadence are unchanged.
  • Prismari Scorched is based on 50% of actual initial hit damage, and small burn ticks correctly respect fire resistance without gaining extra on-hit damage. T4 conduit power values are unchanged pending a separate balance pass.
  • Talent nodes now show one clean image: bevelled unit portraits, round stat medallions or hexagonal mechanic emblems. New shared damage, magic and speed artwork replaces layered badges; ranks sit beneath the icons. Talent effects and saved allocations are unchanged.

Fixes

  • Reduced repeated combat work and improved crowded-battle performance, especially during faster Survival playback. Combat rules and spell power stay the same.
