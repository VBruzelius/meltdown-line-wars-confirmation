Prismari Polish and a New Launcher Layout

Explore Prismari in the redesigned launcher, choose clearer spell effects and enjoy cleaner talent icons and battlefield visuals.

New

  • The launcher has illustrated Prismari news, an always-visible Play/Update area, clearer navigation, rounded buttons and separate launcher/game version labels.
  • Explore Prismari upgrade types and combinations in the launcher and on the website.
  • Choose from eleven Prismari profile avatars and new crystal-cavern mode artwork.

Balance

  • Prismari Scorched is based on 50% of actual initial hit damage. Small burn ticks now respect fire resistance and avoid extra on-hit bonuses.
  • Diamond blade orbits and their variants reach 2.5 cells, matching their visible area. Per-hit damage and attack cadence are unchanged.

Improvements

  • Choose Low, Medium or High spell effects in hub or match Settings. Attack warnings and gameplay timing stay visible at every setting.
  • Shared square Mute music controls restore your previous volume when unmuted.
  • Prismari arenas have carved cavern-stone walls, gold trim, teal inlays, crystal crowns and quartzite tiles.
  • Permafrost Sweep uses a widening frost-mist breath. Unit descriptions are shorter and show Physical, Magic or mixed damage more clearly.
  • Talent nodes use cleaner single-image faces and distinct purpose shapes. Existing effects, allocations and prerequisites stay unchanged.
  • Repeated combat-definition work, floor rendering, floating damage labels and model resources have been optimized. Performance still depends on device and battle density.

Known issues

  • Prismari race talents remain a design proposal; General talents continue to work.
  • Practice Rounds pacing and physical-device combat performance still need further testing.
