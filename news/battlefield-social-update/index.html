Talents, Battlefield & Social Update

Reworked race and General talents add new ways to build your army. Chat with friends during a match, see who is online, and plan Survival defenses with clearer wave information.

New

  • Read and reply to friends during a match. The small chat window keeps your drafts when minimized and shows unread messages.
  • The hub now shows how many unique accounts are online, including players in Survival and PvP.
  • The Survival wave planner shows upcoming enemy types and their strengths so you can plan your defenses.

Balance

  • Warded enemies and Nullweaver bosses now resist 70% of magic damage.
  • Magic-immune enemies also ignore Disease and other magical debuffs.
  • Wraithscythes deal 40% more weapon damage to diseased enemies. Their empowered sweep every 5 seconds adds 3 Shadow damage per Disease stack to each enemy hit.
  • Race and General talents now offer new interactions: bronze gives small stats or useful support upgrades, silver adds tactical twists, and gold changes how a family plays.
  • Veterans can rally their direct neighbors. Maulers build damage across targets, and hooked enemies can take extra damage.
  • Chainmaster hook-path damage increased. The Ballista gold talent adds a one-second impact stun.
  • Quartermaster adds bonus income on every third committed PvP send.
  • Unbroken Souls preserves Revenant charge between targets, then drains it over three inactive seconds.
  • Offensive bronze damage talents grant 5% per rank. Ignite and Disease specialists improve their main damage effect instead of only their weapon hit.
  • Bronze range talents now grant +0.1 cells per rank, up from +0.05.
  • Disease applications now add at least one stack and refresh duration. Plaguebearer basic sprays and catapult poison clouds build stacks too; catapult impacts retain their 1/2/3-stack additions.

Improvements

  • More compact battle selection, clearer wave cards and better alignment across race-themed windows.
  • Unit descriptions now clearly identify defenders that reveal hidden enemies.
  • New family and effect symbols make talent nodes easier to recognize, with colored paths to distinguish branches.
  • Talent Forge keeps race-specific loadouts together on the left, with clearer active builds, darker buttons, stronger frames and faction emblems. Selected talent details stay on the right.
  • Back to Hub in the Talent Forge now asks whether to save, leave without saving, or keep editing when a loadout has unsaved changes.

Fixes

  • Disease death-spread no longer selects hidden enemies unless they are revealed. Existing Disease keeps ticking when an enemy becomes hidden, and area effects can still hit them.
  • Mixed Survival waves now send their different enemy groups together instead of finishing one type before starting the next.
  • Short matches now show a simple no-XP notice instead of listing reward eligibility requirements.
  • Small talent damage bonuses now accumulate correctly through armor and resistance instead of being lost to rounding.
  • Crowded combat spends less time finding targets and processing unaffected enemies. Multiplayer updates also use less server work and bandwidth, improving responsiveness without changing combat rules.
