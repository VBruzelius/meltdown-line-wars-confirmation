Account and privacy information

Development preview — not the final privacy policy. Meltdown Line Wars is currently operated by an independent individual developer.

What we collect

For accounts, we use your username, email address, internal account ID and timestamps. Supabase handles passwords and authentication. Game preferences, progression and match records associated with an account may also be personal data.

Support requests

When you use our support form we store the account name, contact email, issue category and message to help respond. Do not send passwords, codes or sensitive information. We will set a retention schedule before public launch.

Children and families

We aim to make the game accessible to players of different ages. If you are younger and unsure about registration, ask a trusted adult for help. Age-appropriate registration safeguards are being finalized before public release.

Questions and rights

For data access, corrections or deletion, use our support form or write to meltdownlinewars.support@gmail.com. Account changes may require identity verification.

Our complete privacy policy will be published with final operator identification, retention periods, legal bases and processor information before launch.
