Personal testing with a computer

  1. Follow the official AltStore Classic setup guide for Windows (or its Mac guide). Connect and trust your device as instructed.
  2. Download the unsigned IPA above. In AltStore Classic, use the My Apps + button to select it and sign it with your own Apple ID.
  3. Follow any Developer Mode prompt, then open the game and sign in with your usual game account.
  4. Free Apple-ID signing expires after seven days. Refresh through AltStore before it expires. Download newer game builds here and sign them again to update.

Keep your Apple credentials in the signing tool. This website never asks for your Apple password. Public TestFlight installation will require Apple Developer setup and beta review.
